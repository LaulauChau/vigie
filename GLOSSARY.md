# Vigie

Vigie watches a set of sources for content about how people practice AI in software development and data work, and turns the best of each week into a short digest for colleagues.

## Language

### Collecting

**Source**:
A feed, channel or web page that Vigie watches for new content.
_Avoid_: feed (as the general term), site

**Item**:
One piece of content collected from a source: a blog post, a video or a podcast episode.
_Avoid_: article (that is a Kind), info, entry, post, news

**Kind**:
The format of an item: article, video or podcast. It decides how the item's length is shown.
_Avoid_: type, format

### Judging

**Assessment**:
The LLM's judgement of one item: its score, French summary, audience, topic and reason.
_Avoid_: score (for the whole judgement), review, evaluation

**Score**:
The 0–10 number in an assessment saying how useful the item is to the company.

**Topic**:
One of a small, configured set of themes an item is classified under, used to keep a digest varied.
_Avoid_: subject, category, sujet

**Audience**:
The roles an item is useful for (Dev, Data, Tech lead, Cellule IA, Manager), shown as "Pour qui".
_Avoid_: target, persona

### Publishing

**Window**:
The period of time a digest covers, seven days by default.
_Avoid_: period, range

**Duplicate**:
An item telling the same story as another item in the window, such as one announcement covered by two blogs. Only the better of the two can become a pick.
_Avoid_: same subject, same topic

**Shortlist**:
The best-scored items of a window, compared against each other to choose the picks.
_Avoid_: candidates, top 10

**Pick**:
An item chosen to appear in a digest.
_Avoid_: selection, highlight, info

**Digest**:
The weekly message of at most five picks, posted to Slack.
_Avoid_: newsletter, report, roundup
